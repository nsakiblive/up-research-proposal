05 / Working approach

A defined process.
A documented result.

From the initial question to evidence that your team can review and use.

  1. 01

    Define the requirement

    Clarify the business question, intended use, research boundaries and expected deliverables.

  2. 02

    Collect & verify

    Identify credible sources, document the collection method and flag missing or uncertain information.

  3. 03

    Analyse & organise

    Structure the evidence, compare relevant indicators and develop supported observations.

  4. 04

    Deliver & document

    Provide the agreed dataset, findings, references and methodological notes for review.

Accuracy, confidentiality and practical usability guide the work at each stage.
